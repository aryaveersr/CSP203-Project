CampusHub

Unifying Academics & Campus Community in One Place

A centralized platform built for IIT Bhilai students to streamline daily coursework, track assignments, and never miss out on campus club events.

The Problem

Information Scattered Everywhere

Life at IIT Bhilai moves fast. Between lab submissions, tutorial problem sets, quizzes, and a packed calendar of technical workshops, SciTech fests, and cultural nights, important updates get buried across dozens of different channels. Students either miss an assignment deadline or find out about a great club session after it has already ended.

Our Solution

Academics & Community Unified

We designed CampusHub around the two pillars of student life: Academics and Community. By unifying an Assignment & Homework Tracker with a live Campus Event & Club Board, students can manage their study priorities alongside CoSA club events, GDG bootcamps, and OpenLake sprints in one clean interface.
